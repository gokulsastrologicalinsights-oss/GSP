Connect YouTube Channel

Google Cloud credentials configured. Sign in with your Google Account to connect your YouTube Channel. Never enter Google passwords into third-party forms.

▶️ Continue with Google (for YouTube)

Enter your Google Cloud Console OAuth 2.0 Client ID and Client Secret. Never shared publicly.

Paste into: APIs & Services → Credentials → OAuth 2.0 Client IDs → Authorized redirect URIs

Or connect directly with a YouTube Channel ID and OAuth Access Token (e.g. from Google OAuth 2.0 Playground).

Requires scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl

Select YouTube Channel
